What Coconut Creek callers usually need from us

Most calls from Coconut Creek fall into a few groups. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for all of them.

Leaks that will not stop

Pipes that split, fittings that let go and supply lines that fail get same-day attention when we have a crew free, and phone help right away. See burst pipe repair for what happens on site.

Drains and backups

Slow sinks, overflowing tubs and gurgling toilets are usually a cable job, and a main line backup needs sewer line cleaning.

Water heater trouble

No hot water at night is a plumbing emergency for a family. We repair or replace tanks and can discuss tankless units when the time comes.

Hidden leaks and high bills

A meter that keeps moving points to a leak somewhere. Leak detection traces it without opening more of your walls than needed.

Before we arrive in Coconut Creek: steps for an active leak

The next few minutes matter more than the next few hours. These steps keep damage small while a plumber is on the way.

Close the main valve

Find it near the water heater, at the garage wall, or beside the meter box in the yard. Turn it fully to the closed position.

Kill power near wet areas

Switch off breakers for rooms with standing water only if you can reach the panel dry. Otherwise stay out and tell us on the phone.

Clear the floor

Lift rugs, move electronics and set towels or a bucket where water is falling. Photos taken now help with any insurance claim.

Call the 24/7 line

Call (561) 448-8545, tell us your street and what you see, and we will take it from there.

Who supplies your water and sewer, and where we come in

Coconut Creek buys its water from Broward County and distributes it through city-maintained lines. For wastewater, Broward County provides transmission and treatment, while the city maintains local wastewater infrastructure. Properties south of Coconut Creek Parkway are served by the City of Margate instead.

This matters when something goes wrong. A leak or backup on the utility's side of your property line is the utility's to fix, and a problem on your side is ours. If you are not sure which side the problem is on, call us and describe it, because the quickest way to find out is to ask, and we will tell you plainly when it belongs to the utility.

If you live south of Coconut Creek Parkway, check with the City of Margate for utility questions, then call us for work inside your property. Our water service line repair page explains what the line between the meter and the house involves.
