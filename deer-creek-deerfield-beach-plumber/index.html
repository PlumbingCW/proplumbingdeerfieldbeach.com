What Deer Creek is and how it is organized

Deer Creek shows up on the City of Deerfield Beach commission districts map as its own named neighborhood. Published guides describe an 18-hole golf course, small lakes, and housing that includes condos, townhouses and single-family homes. Hillsboro Boulevard and Deer Creek Boulevard are the roads people mention most.

The community is divided into many named sections, and a Deer Creek Improvement Association exists. We do not claim to know which rules apply on your street, so check with your own association.

Sorting out who handles a plumbing problem

With so many sections, a short checklist avoids wasted calls.

  1. 1

    Stop the water if it is spreading

    Close the shut-off under the fixture or at the unit or house main. If you do not know where it is, call us and we will talk you through finding it.

  2. 2

    Look at where the water is

    A leak at a faucet, toilet or water heater inside your home is almost always yours to fix. Water coming from a wall shared with a neighbor or from a common area is a different matter.

  3. 3

    Tell the association if a shared line may be involved

    Managers and boards often have their own plumbers for common lines and want to know quickly. Ask what they require before outside work starts.

  4. 4

    Call us for anything inside your walls

    We can look at the problem, tell you what we see and explain the repair, so you can decide before work begins.

Jobs we see in a golf and lake community

Lakes and fairways make Deer Creek pleasant, and they shape the plumbing in a few practical ways.

Irrigation and outdoor valves

Outdoor valves and watering lines work hard around lawns and landscaping. A wet patch that does not dry is worth a look.

Water heater age

Look at the data plate on the tank for a date. If it is old, plan the replacement before it fails at an inconvenient time.

Fixtures and toilets

Running toilets, dripping faucets and worn supply lines are the small jobs that add up on a water bill. Toilet repair covers the usual fixes.
