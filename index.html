Emergencies

Emergency plumber calls we take at any hour

An emergency plumber is only useful if the phone gets answered at 2 a.m. and a plumber is dispatched. These are the situations where waiting for business hours costs you money.

  • A pipe is spraying or flooding. Close the main valve and call. Our burst pipe repair page lists what to do in the first minutes.
  • Sewage or several drains backing up. Stop using water and call. A main line problem is the likely cause, and sewer line cleaning clears it.
  • No hot water, or a tank leaking. A leaking tank usually means water heater replacement, while a tripped control or a failed element is often a repair.
  • A toilet that will not stop overflowing. Close the supply valve behind the bowl, then call for toilet repair.
  • A water meter that spins with everything off. That points to a hidden leak, and leak detection finds it before the bill grows.
  • Water coming through a ceiling or wall. Shut off the main valve, move belongings and call right away. Tell us if you live in a condo so we can plan around neighbors.
  • The smell of gas. Leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first. Gas leaks are not a plumber-first call.

Our emergency plumber line is staffed around the clock, including holidays. We cannot promise an exact arrival time, but we give you our honest estimate when you call and walk you through safe steps while you wait.

Local

Plumbing in a coastal city: what Deerfield Beach properties deal with

Salt air near Main Beach and the International Fishing Pier attacks exposed metal fittings, outdoor spigots and garage plumbing. If you live close to the water, a yearly look at exposed valves can catch corrosion before it turns into a leak.

Many Deerfield Beach buildings are condos or planned communities, including Century Village, Hillsboro Cove and Crystal Lake. Shared stacks and association rules often decide who fixes what, so ask your manager before work that touches shared lines.

The city draws its drinking water from groundwater and treats it with chloramine. That is a good reason to ask any water filter seller whether their system suits chloramine, and a good reason to test your own tap before buying a softener.

Along W Hillsboro Blvd, shops and restaurants in strips such as Deer Run and Shoppes at Deerfield depend on floor drains and grease lines that need regular attention. Our commercial plumbing service covers after-hours calls for those businesses.
