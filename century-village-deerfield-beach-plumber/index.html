About Century Village

The Deerfield Beach Historical Society says Century Village opened in 1979 on 800 acres, with 8,508 units offered for sale and marketed to people over 55. Real estate listings describe it as gated, and the City of Deerfield Beach shows it on its commission districts map.

Streets near the community include Century Boulevard, West Hillsboro Boulevard, Powerline Road and Southwest 10th Street. Buildings carry names such as Oakridge, Tilford, Markham, Prescott and Newport, so give your building name when you call.

Water leaking in your unit or from the unit above

A leak in a condo can reach more than one home, so a few quick steps help.

Shut off the unit's water

Close the shut-off for your unit, or the one under the leaking fixture. If you cannot find it, call us and we will describe where it usually is.

Tell the building office

Managers and boards often need to know quickly, especially if water may be reaching neighbors.

Protect and photograph

Move belongings, soak up standing water and take dated photos for your insurer.

Call us for the repair

Describe where the water is. We can look at the source and explain what failed.

Fixtures and appliances we work on inside units

Most calls from condo owners are for things inside the unit itself.

Toilets

Running, leaking and weak-flush toilets are common. See toilet repair for the usual fixes.

Kitchen and bath faucets

Drips and low flow often come from worn cartridges or supply lines. Faucet and sink repair covers the options.

Water heaters

Look at the data plate for a date and check the base for rust or moisture. Read water heater replacement if yours is old.

Garbage disposals

A jammed or leaking disposal can often be fixed or swapped. See garbage disposal repair.

Working inside a gated condominium community

Gates, building offices and shared hallways mean access takes a little planning. Tell us how to reach your building and whether the association needs notice, and we will plan around it.

In a community of this size, how water is billed and which lines belong to the owner can differ by building. Your association or the City of Deerfield Beach can tell you how yours works.
