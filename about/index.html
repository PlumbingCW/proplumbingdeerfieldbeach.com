About Deerfield Beach Pro Plumbing

We are a plumbing team based in Deerfield Beach and built around one idea: when plumbing fails, the phone should be answered and the price should be clear.

Who we are

Deerfield Beach Pro Plumbing dispatches licensed and insured plumbers from 3256 W Hillsboro Blvd. We serve homes, condos and small businesses in Deerfield Beach and nearby Broward County towns.

Our emergency line is open 24 hours a day, every day. We offer same-day appointments when available and never promise an arrival time we cannot keep.

How we work

Clear price before work starts

We explain what we found, what the options are and what each costs. Nothing begins until you agree.

Honest repair or replace advice

If a repair will hold, we say so. If a part is at the end of its life, we explain why replacing it saves trouble.

Respect for shared buildings

In condos and managed communities we work with your association rules and tell you when the building needs to be involved.

Plain answers

We describe problems in everyday words so you can decide with confidence.

What we handle

Our work covers burst pipes, drain cleaning, leak detection, water heater repair, sewer line repair, fixtures and commercial plumbing. The full list is on our services page.

To the west, we also serve Coconut Creek, and our service areas page lists the other towns we take calls from.

What a visit looks like

Every job follows the same simple path, whether it is a midnight burst pipe or a planned fixture swap.

  1. 1

    You call and describe the problem

    We ask a few questions about what you see, hear and smell. If water is running, we help you shut it off first.

  2. 2

    We look before we quote

    The plumber checks the fixture, the lines and the area around it, then tells you what is wrong in plain words.

  3. 3

    You choose the repair

    We lay out the options and the cost of each. Work starts only after you agree.

  4. 4

    We finish and explain

    The plumber tests the repair, cleans up the work area and tells you what to watch for afterward.

Emergency calls and planned work

Some problems cannot wait. A burst line, a sewage backup or a water heater leaking onto the floor needs a plumber now, and that is what the 24/7 line is for.

Other jobs can be planned, such as a water heater installation or a new fixture. For those, we schedule a time that suits you and talk through the options ahead of the visit.

Commercial customers get the same approach. Restaurants, offices and retail units often need work scheduled around opening hours, so tell us when the space is quiet and we will plan the visit for then. Our commercial plumbing page explains what we handle for businesses.

If you are unsure which kind of problem you have, call and describe it. We will tell you honestly whether it should be handled tonight or can safely wait until morning.

Plumbing in Deerfield Beach homes

Many homes and condos in the area sit on concrete slabs, so supply and drain lines often run under the floor. A leak in those lines can stay hidden for weeks, which is why we start with listening gear and meter checks before opening anything.

The city's water report says its supply comes from groundwater in the Biscayne and Floridan aquifers and is disinfected with chloramine. We mention it because it is a useful detail to share with anyone installing a filter or softener.

For that kind of project, our water softener and filtration page explains what to ask before you buy.

How to reach us

The fastest way to reach a plumber is to call (561) 448-8545, any hour of the day. If your question is not urgent, you can also email contact@proplumbingdeerfieldbeach.com and we will reply during business hours.

Our office is at 3256 W Hillsboro Blvd in Deerfield Beach, and our contact page has a map. Please call before visiting, since our plumbers spend most of the day out on jobs.

Check any plumber's license

Whoever you hire, look up the license on the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation online services first. Under Florida law an unlicensed contractor cannot enforce its contract, so the check protects you.

We are licensed and insured, and we are glad to talk you through how to verify any plumber.
