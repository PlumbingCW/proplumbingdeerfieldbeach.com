Plumbing Service Areas Around Deerfield Beach

We are based at 3256 W Hillsboro Blvd in Deerfield Beach and send plumbers across northeast Broward County. This page shows where we work and which towns have their own page.

Where our plumbers go

Our home base is Deerfield Beach, ZIP 33442, on W Hillsboro Blvd. From there we cover neighborhoods across the city, from the beach side to the western communities, and we dispatch to nearby towns when a call comes in.

Deerfield Beach itself is served from the pages across this site, including drain cleaning, water heater repair and leak detection. Coconut Creek, our neighbor to the west, has its own page with local details and emergency steps.

Town pages

Coconut Creek

Emergency plumbing for Coconut Creek homes and businesses, with ZIP codes 33097, 33073, 33063 and 33066. See the Coconut Creek page.

What to tell us when you call

Give the street address and ZIP code first, then describe what the water or drain is doing. A short description such as a burst pipe under a sink, a backed-up floor drain or a leaking water heater is enough for us to plan the visit.

If water is running and you can reach the main shutoff, close it before you dial. That protects floors and belongings while we get to you, and it keeps the problem from growing.

Other nearby places we get calls from

We also take calls from Hillsboro Beach, Lighthouse Point, Pompano Beach, Boca Raton and Boynton Beach. These do not have separate pages yet, but you can call and ask whether we can reach your address.

Coverage depends on crew location and how urgent the problem is, so the fastest way to confirm is to call (561) 448-8545 and give the street and ZIP.
