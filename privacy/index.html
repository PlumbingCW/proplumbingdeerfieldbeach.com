Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026. This policy explains what information we handle when you call us or visit this website.

Calls to our line

When you call us, we ask for your name, address and a description of the problem so we can schedule service. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

We use call details only to provide and improve our service.

This website

Our website does not have forms or accounts, so we do not collect information you type into the site. Like most sites, our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

Embedded maps are provided by Google and may set their own cookies under Google's policies.

How we share information

We share information only with the plumbers and staff who need it to complete your job, and with service providers who help us operate the business. We share it with authorities only when the law requires.

You can ask us what we hold about you or ask us to delete it by calling or emailing us.

Contact us about privacy

Questions about this policy can go to contact@proplumbingdeerfieldbeach.com or (561) 448-8545. We may update this policy from time to time, and the date above shows the latest version.
