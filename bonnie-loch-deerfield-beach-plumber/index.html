What to do when one drain keeps clogging

A single stubborn drain usually follows a pattern, and a few steps help us find the cause.

  1. 1

    Note which fixtures are affected

    One slow sink points to its own trap or branch line. Several slow fixtures point to the main line.

  2. 2

    Skip harsh chemicals

    Chemical cleaners can damage pipes and make later repairs more hazardous for a plumber.

  3. 3

    Try a plunger on single clogs

    A plunger solves many clogs in a toilet or sink. If it fails twice, stop and call.

  4. 4

    Ask about a camera look

    When a line clogs again and again, a sewer camera inspection shows what is going on inside the pipe.

Homes with shared pools and community rules

Guides mention that many communities in the area have associations with pools and tennis courts. Rules differ from one community to the next, so ask yours what it expects before exterior plumbing work.

Inside your own home, the repair is yours to arrange, and we are glad to talk through options by phone first.
